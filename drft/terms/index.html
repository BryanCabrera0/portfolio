2. What DRFT does

DRFT is a wake-word listener for iPhone and Mac. You pick the words that matter — your name, a project code, a topic — and DRFT alerts you when those words come up in calls, meetings, or conversations happening around you. AI-generated summaries are a Pro feature.

On iPhone, DRFT uses the device microphone to hear what is audible in the room — designed for speakerphone calls and in-person conversations, not a direct tap into the call line. On Mac, DRFT uses Core Audio process taps to capture the audio output of other applications on your system (Zoom, Teams, Meet, etc.) — no microphone is involved. On both platforms, speech recognition runs on-device. Audio is processed in real time and discarded immediately — never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

5. Recording consent & ambient audio — your responsibility

DRFT does not record calls in the traditional sense — it processes audio in real time solely for wake-word detection, and no audio is saved at any point. However, because DRFT listens to ambient audio (iPhone) and to the audio output of conferencing apps (Mac), those streams may include conversation content, which means monitoring and consent laws may apply.

On Mac specifically, Core Audio process taps can capture the audio output of apps including video-conferencing tools. That audio may include the voices of remote participants on a call. DRFT processes this audio transiently on-device and does not record or store it, but you should be aware that the audio is being processed for wake-word detection.
