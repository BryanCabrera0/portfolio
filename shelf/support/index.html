Support

GET HELP WITH SHELF FOR iPHONE

1. Contact

Email support@drft.ing. Shelf is built by 1 person — Bryan Cabrera — who personally responds to every message, usually within 1–2 business days.

When reporting a bug, include:

  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • What you were doing when the issue happened
  • A screenshot, if it helps show the problem

2. The basics

  • Add food — Tap the camera button and snap your food. The photo is cut out on-device and lands on the shelf as a sticker. Give it a name and a date, and the wilt begins.
  • Eat it — Drag a sticker up to peel it off the shelf. That's the whole gesture — the peel is the celebration.
  • Change a name or date — Tap a sticker to open it, then edit the name or pick a new date. Your edits always win; Smart ID never overwrites them.
  • Nudge time — Shelf sends one quiet local notification on a sticker's last good day. Pick when it arrives in settings.

3. Smart ID (shelf+)

With shelf+ and the Smart ID toggle on, snapping food names it and sets its expiry clock automatically. If Smart ID can't reach the network or times out, the sticker still lands — just unnamed and without a clock; tap it to fill both in. The settings toggle turns Smart ID off entirely, and your photos never leave the iPhone while it's off. Free-tier identification is always 100% on-device.

4. Subscription

  • Manage or cancel — Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or “manage subscription” inside the App. Apple handles all billing.
  • Restore purchases — On the shelf+ screen, tap restore purchases.
  • Refunds — Handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Lapsed? — Nothing is deleted. Every sticker stays, wilts, and can be eaten; new captures gate at the 9-sticker cap until you resubscribe.

5. Shared shelf (shelf+)

Share your shelf from settings; invitees join free with an iPhone signed into iCloud, and their captures land on your shelf. You can stop sharing — and members can leave — at any time from the same sheet. One joined shelf at a time; up to 100 participants.

6. Your data

Everything lives on your iPhone — there are no accounts and no Shelf server. Deleting the app deletes your data. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

7. Requirements

Shelf requires an iPhone running iOS 26 or later. The free tier works fully offline; Smart ID and shared shelf need a network connection.
