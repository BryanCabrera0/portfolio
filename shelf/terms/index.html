2. What Shelf does

Shelf is a food expiry tracker for iPhone. You snap a photo of food, the photo becomes a die-cut sticker on a virtual shelf, and the sticker wilts in real time until you eat it (peel it off) or it expires. Shelf tracks when food goes bad — it is not an inventory manager, shopping list, or recipe app, and it has no accounts of any kind.

3. Free and shelf+ tiers

Shelf offers 2 service tiers:

  • Free tier — The full core loop: snap, name, clock, wilt, eat. Up to 9 stickers on the shelf at a time. Fully offline; ad-free.
  • shelf+ — A shelf that never fills (no sticker cap); Smart ID (the photo is named and clocked automatically); shared shelf (share with up to 100 people, who join free); savings stats. Pricing: $1.99/month or $9.99/year. The 7-day free trial applies to the yearly plan only.

If your subscription lapses, nothing is deleted. Every sticker stays visible, wilts, and notifies; eating and composting always work. New captures gate at the 9-sticker cap again until you resubscribe.

5. Food safety — your judgment, not Shelf's

Shelf's wilt timers run on the dates you (or Smart ID) set, and Smart ID expiry estimates are AI-generated approximations based on typical shelf life — they do not know how your food was stored, handled, or packaged.

Shelf is a reminder tool, not a food-safety authority. A fresh-looking sticker does not mean food is safe to eat, and a wilted sticker does not always mean it is spoiled. Always rely on your own senses, the printed date on the packaging, and official food-safety guidance before eating anything. You are solely responsible for decisions about what you eat.
